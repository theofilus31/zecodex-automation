Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

ZECODEX Automation ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the ZECODEX YouTube channel to manage that channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube account and channel information (channel name, ID, subscriber count), to confirm the correct channel is connected.
  • YouTube Analytics data for the connected channel (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, audience country, age and gender in aggregate), to plan content.
  • Video upload access, to upload videos the channel owner has approved.
  • Public YouTube data (search results and public video statistics), to identify trending topics.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to analyse and manage the owner's own channel.
  • All data and the OAuth token are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on a server operated by the app.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Analytics data is refreshed regularly and kept only as long as needed for the owner's reporting.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer read or upload anything. Locally stored data can be deleted by removing the app's data folder.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
