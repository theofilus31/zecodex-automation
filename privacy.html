Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

ZECODEX Automation ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the ZECODEX YouTube channel to manage that channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used, stored, shared and deleted.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube account and channel information (channel name, ID, subscriber count), to confirm the correct channel is connected.
  • YouTube Analytics data for the connected channel (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, audience country, age and gender in aggregate), to plan content.
  • Video upload access, to upload videos the channel owner has approved.
  • Public YouTube data (search results and public video statistics), to identify trending topics and to measure when the channel's own videos gain views.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to analyse and manage the owner's own channel and is shown only to the channel owner.
  • The app computes one derived metric, "views per hour", from hourly snapshots of the public view count of the channel's own videos. It is labelled in the app as an estimate calculated by the app, not a YouTube metric.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Storage and retention

  • All data and the OAuth token are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on a server operated by the app.
  • Public YouTube data of other channels (trend research) and per-fetch snapshots are deleted after 30 days.
  • The channel's own Analytics data is refreshed daily; rows older than the refreshed window (90 days) are deleted.
  • Hourly view-count snapshots are kept for 30 days.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer read or upload anything. All YouTube API data the app stored is deleted within 30 days of revocation, or immediately on request by deleting the app's local data folder (state and secrets).
